AURORA HACK

A 48-hour AI hackathon in London for women & non-binary engineers, designers, and founders. Build the next generation of AI — no prior hackathon experience needed.

THEME

AI × POWER

Aurora hack illuminates what's possible - a 48 hour hackathon where students discover their power, founders test bold ideas and builders solve real life problems - all learning, building and rising together.

Each track represents a different stage of ambition: those discovering their power (students), those building it (founders), those scaling it (early-stage), and those defining it (open). Together, they form the ecosystem.

TRACKS
01

ASCENT

Identify one annoying, repetitive problem, yours, your school's, or your community's, and build an AI solution for it in 48 hours. No experience required. Examples: a chatbot that answers the same FAQs over and over, an automation that handles admin drudgery, a classifier that sorts things no one wants to sort manually, an agent that schedules or coordinates tasks. The constraint: it has to solve something real that wastes time or frustrates people. Focus on execution and clarity, not polish.

Judging criteria: Does it work? Does it actually solve the problem? Did you ship it?

First place £? + feature in Aurora Hack alumni spotlight + invitation to mentor next year's cohort

Runner-up £? + 3-month access to [Sponsor] API credits

Special prize (voted by mentors) "Best First Build" — £? + [Sponsor] swag pack + feature on Aurora Hack socials

Sponsor angle: Reach future engineers at the exact moment they're learning. Ascent winners become brand ambassadors, they remember who supported them. Plus: direct recruitment funnel into your grad program or internships.

02

IGNITION

(For founders with an idea + early-stage startups)

Build something live in 48 hours.

If you're a founder with an idea: prove it works. Ship an MVP or prototype, then pitch it to VCs in 3 minutes.

If you're an early-stage startup: ship a new feature that moves your product forward, or build a prototype of your next big feature and gather user feedback.

Your deliverable: a working demo + a short pitch (founders: 3 min pitch deck; early-stage: 5-min case study of what you built and why it matters).

Why "Ignition"? Whether you're sparking an idea or scaling momentum, this is where execution happens. This is what fundable looks like.

First place £? + 20-minute 1-on-1 with [Lead VC Sponsor] + consideration for accelerator programs

Runner-up £? + intro calls with [VC Sponsor] + [Partner] credits

Special prize (voted by VCs + mentors) "Most Fundable" — £? + meeting with [Sponsor] investor network

Sponsor angle: Direct pipeline to founders at all stages - pre-seed ideas and early-stage teams. You see fast execution and get first look at the best opportunities.

03

DAYBREAK

(Build AI that lights the way)

Use AI to solve real problems. Teams building projects in accessibility, climate, education, health, social impact, or any domain where AI can make a tangible difference. Your goal: a working prototype that demonstrates impact, whether that's a tool that helps, a system that scales, or a model that proves the concept.

First place £? + feature in Aurora Hack impact spotlight + [Impact Partner] grant consideration

Runner-up £? + [Sponsor] credits / API access

Special prize (voted by judges + community) "Most Meaningful Impact" — £? + speaking slot at [Sponsor] event + press feature

Sponsor angle: Brand association with purpose-driven tech. Show your commitment to impact, reach builders solving real problems, and find teams aligned with your mission.

ELIGIBILITY

Sixth form students

are open to enter any track (Ascent, Ignition or Daybreak). Ascent is tailored for you, but if you have a start up idea or an impact project, go where it fits.

Everyone else

Ascent is sixth-form focused, but if you are mentoring or want to experience it, join. Daybreak and Ignition are open to all.

SHARED ACROSS ALL TRACKS

  • Clarity (can you explain it?)

  • Impact (does it matter?)
  • Use of time (did you ship fast?)

Judges panel: Mix of VCs, founders (and invited guests), and tech leads from sponsor companies.
